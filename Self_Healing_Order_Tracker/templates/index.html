Reliability definition

MTTD: Average milliseconds from fault injection to detection, shown in seconds.

MTTR: Average milliseconds from fault injection to recovered state, shown in seconds.

Recovery success rate: Successfully recovered incidents ÷ total incidents × 100. Timing values are measured from persisted incident timestamps, not fixed demo numbers.
